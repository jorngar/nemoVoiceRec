## Design Context

### Users
The owner wants a desktop voice recorder that transcribes conversations and clearly distinguishes speakers. Personal meeting/interview use is the provisional audience, pending their answer.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, familiar. The user's explicit reference is the standard macOS recorder, including its looks and recording features.

### Aesthetic Direction
Native macOS Voice Memos: warm light gray sidebar, white workspace, system typography, fine dividers, red recording control. Speaker labels use consistent colors, numbers, and editable names; never color alone.

### Design Principles
1. Recording is always one obvious action.
2. Preserve source audio and represent uncertain speaker attribution honestly.
3. Keep the transcript readable and synchronized with playback.
4. Favor native conventions, keyboard access, restrained motion, and generous whitespace.
5. Technical model setup belongs in settings, outside the daily recording flow.
